## 1. Executive summary

- Thinkery uses a land-and-expand monetization system.
- Community Kits create free adoption and reusable distribution.
- Paid Kits monetize proprietary context and higher-value workflows.
- Platform subscriptions monetize governance, reliability, and collaboration.
- Usage charges monetize compute and verified operational activity.
- Partner revenue extends reach without matching direct sales headcount.
- Services accelerate proof, implementation, and enterprise conversion.
- Free distribution is a funnel input, not a promise of free support.
- Enterprise value is tied to savings, risk reduction, and time-to-answer.
- The base case reaches approximately CHF 9.7 million Year 3 revenue.
- Year 1 prioritizes pilots and reference customers.
- Year 2 adds repeatable platform sales and marketplace liquidity.
- Year 3 scales enterprise contracts and certified partners.
- Target blended gross margin reaches 77% in Year 3.
- Target net revenue retention reaches 115% by the end of Year 3.
- Target blended CAC payback remains below 12 months.
- Services are used to create product pull, not to hide weak retention.
- Marketplace take rate defaults to 25% of third-party kit sales.
- Savings share applies only to auditable and customer-approved savings.
- Usage pricing remains transparent and low enough to encourage expansion.
- Enterprise pricing protects deployment, support, and governance costs.
- Partner economics reward sourced demand and reusable delivery assets.
- Every contract has a measurable activation milestone.
- Every paid cohort has a retention and expansion owner.

## 4. Pricing principles

- Price for recurring value, not implementation effort alone.
- Keep free access sufficient for genuine product discovery.
- Keep Team pricing low enough for bottoms-up adoption.
- Make Business pricing simple enough for a manager to approve.
- Make Enterprise pricing flexible enough for deployment variation.
- Separate fixed platform value from variable usage value.
- Charge for private and proprietary context.
- Charge for governance when governance creates audit value.
- Charge for services when expert labor is consumed.
- Reward annual commitment with cash-flow-friendly discounts.
- Preserve a clear upgrade path at every limit.
- Avoid artificial limits that block evaluation.
- Use hard limits only when cost or risk is real.
- Show estimated bills before usage is incurred.
- Provide usage alerts at 50%, 80%, and 100% of budget.
- Require approval for savings-share calculations.
- Avoid double billing the same value metric.
- Let customers choose predictable or performance-linked pricing.
- Publish a reference rate card for partner trust.
- Review prices after evidence of willingness to pay.

## 12. Unit economics framework

### 12.1 Definitions

- CAC: fully loaded acquisition cost per new paying logo.
- S&M cost includes sales, marketing, events, and sales tools.
- Onboarding cost is separated from CAC where possible.
- Gross margin: revenue minus cost of revenue divided by revenue.
- Contribution margin: gross profit minus account-specific support cost.
- LTV: expected gross-profit contribution over customer lifetime.
- Logo churn: lost paying logos divided by opening logos.
- Revenue churn: lost recurring revenue divided by opening recurring revenue.
- Expansion: additional recurring revenue from existing accounts.
- NRR: opening recurring revenue plus expansion minus contraction and churn.
- GRR: opening recurring revenue minus contraction and churn.
- Payback: CAC divided by monthly contribution margin.
- ARPA: average recurring revenue per account.
- ACV: annualized recurring revenue plus committed recurring usage.
- Blended figures use the revenue mix in the base forecast.

### 12.2 Unit economics formula sheet

- Monthly gross profit = monthly revenue × gross margin.
- Monthly contribution = monthly gross profit − account support cost.
- Simple LTV = monthly ARPA × gross margin ÷ monthly logo churn.
- Contribution LTV = monthly contribution ÷ monthly logo churn.
- CAC payback months = CAC ÷ monthly contribution.
- NRR = (opening recurring + expansion − contraction − churn) ÷ opening recurring.
- GRR = (opening recurring − contraction − churn) ÷ opening recurring.
- Magic number = net new ARR × 4 ÷ prior-quarter S&M spend.
- Pipeline coverage = qualified pipeline ÷ target new ARR.
- Services conversion = platform wins from services ÷ delivered services accounts.
- Marketplace take rate = commission revenue ÷ marketplace GMV.
- Savings share rate = savings-share revenue ÷ proven savings.
- Revenue per delivery FTE = services revenue ÷ delivery FTE.
- Gross profit per support FTE = gross profit ÷ support FTE.
